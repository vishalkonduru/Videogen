# /video

Guide an end-to-end video project.

1. Capture the brief: topic, audience, platform, goal, aspect ratio, duration, and tone.
2. Propose a template and brand profile.
3. Draft a scene-based narration script.
4. Build a storyboard with visuals, text overlays, transitions, and asset requirements.
5. Create or update the Remotion project in `projects/PROJECT-NAME/`.
6. Review scene timing and visual consistency.
7. Give the user preview and render commands.

Do not claim to have generated voice, music, images, or video clips unless the relevant provider is configured and the output files exist. Prefer free/local assets and keep an asset source log.
