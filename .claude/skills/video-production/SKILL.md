# Video production skill

## Core principles
- One video, one central promise.
- Hook quickly, then build a clear sequence of ideas.
- Narration should sound natural when read aloud.
- Every shot must communicate something; avoid filler visuals.
- Design for the target platform and viewing distance.
- Use captions and on-screen text sparingly and legibly.
- Keep a record of asset origins and licenses.

## Scene schema
Each scene should define:
- `title`: short internal label
- `narration`: spoken words
- `visual`: visible action or composition
- `durationInSeconds`: estimated duration
- `assets`: optional local asset paths

## Quality checks
Verify factual claims, spelling, audio clarity, scene timing, aspect ratio, contrast, safe margins, and export playback before delivery.
